# Travel bags and cases: Brompton 16" and 20" (G Line / T20)

**Created:** 5 Oct 2026
**⚠️ Separate list. These weights and prices are NOT part of any bike build total** in `T20_Build_Comparison.md` or `T20_Build_Plan.md`.

The T20 is a G Line clone, so it takes the **20" (G Line)** bags. The 16" section covers classic A/C/P/T Line Bromptons (for example your wife's P Line).

## Carry-on vs check-in: the short answer

- **Carry-on is unlikely for either size.** A folded 16" Brompton (~58.5 × 56.5 × 27cm) is already bigger than a typical cabin limit (~56 × 45 × 25cm), and the G Line / T20 fold is bigger again. A few airlines let it through, but don't plan on it.
- **Transport covers** (Brompton Transit Transport Bag) are for **trains, cars and the overhead bin if allowed**. Brompton says they're not for checked baggage.
- **Check-in** needs a padded soft bag or a hard case. The 20" cases measure **~187-202cm linear** (length + width + height), over the usual **158cm** checked-bag limit, so expect sports-equipment or oversize rules. Check your airline.
- **Weight budget:** a T20 (~8.5-9kg) in a 5-10kg case is ~14-19kg, under the usual 23kg economy limit.

## Picks

- **20" / T20:** **Vincita Sightseer for G Line (5.2kg, ~US$239)** for flying: half the weight of the B&W XL and folds away at the destination. **B&W Foldon XL** if you want maximum protection and don't mind 10kg. **Brompton G Line Transit bag (1.16kg, £85)** for trains and cars.
- **16":** **Vincita Sightseer 4.0 (3.8kg, US$290)**, or the Brompton Transit Transport Bag (~450g) as a train cover.
