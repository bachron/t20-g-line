# HEXATI T20 — Purchase Order & Cost Tracker

**Created 25 Sep 2026.** The tracker weighs this build to the gram but has never totalled its
cost, so there was no way to answer *"what does this bike cost"* or *"what do I buy first."*
This is that view: every part as a line item, priced where the research has a figure, with an
explicit list of what is still unpriced.

Prices are as tracked in `T20_Build_Plan.md` / `T20_Build_Options.md`, converted at the rates
those docs already use (**US$1 ≈ S$1.30, €1 ≈ S$1.47, £1 ≈ S$1.74, ¥100 ≈ S$0.88**). They are
research figures, not quotes — treat ±10% as normal and re-check before paying.

📌 **Context worth stating once.** A complete T20 Lite from Chris Yeo is **S$3,200**; from
JackBikeSG **S$3,350**. You have already committed S$2,900 on the frameset alone, and the priced
parts add nearly S$2,000 on top — **before** the wheelset, crankset, fork, tyres, rotors, levers,
cassette and rack are priced at all. This build will land at a **multiple of the complete bike's
price**, which is normal for a parts-built lightweight and is the cost of going from 9.4kg to
~7.9-8.4kg. It is not a reason to change course — but the number should be on paper, not implied.

💡 **Cross-reference § Value tiers** in `T20_Build_Options.md` before adding anything. The cheapest
weight in the whole build is **ESI foam grips: ~175g for budget money.** The most expensive is the
THM crank at **~S$23/g**.

## 6. Buy order

### Wave 1 — now, genuinely unblocked
Bottom bracket · seatpost · grips · chainring bolts
→ **~S$498 – 514.** Nothing about these depends on any open question.

⚠️ **The spider (S$250) and chainring (S$195) are NOT in Wave 1 any more.** Both hang off the
drivetrain route: the 2× option makes the 1× spider the wrong part, and the smaller-ring options
make the 48T the wrong ring. **S$445 of re-buy risk** if they're ordered before the route is
settled. They move to Wave 3 with the route decision.

### Wave 2 — the moment Chris Yeo answers
1. **Axle standard** → wheelset + dropout hanger *(the two most expensive blocked items)*
2. **Caliper mount** → calipers + rotor size
3. **Headset pattern** → headset
4. **Rack mounts** → rack
5. **Bar clamp diameter** → handlebar

### Wave 3 — your decisions, no external dependency
**The drivetrain route first**, then spider · chainring · cassette · chain (or reuse) · saddle · pedals

> ⚠️ **Settle the drivetrain route before Wave 3, not during it.** eR9 caps the cassette at 11-32T, which
> makes the low gear **1.50:1** instead of **0.96:1** — and the 48T chainring in Wave 1 was chosen
> *specifically* for that 0.96:1. eTX (11-50T capable, flat-bar, +~120g) keeps it. The chainring
> and cassette both hang off this, so deciding it late means re-buying. **The 48T ring in Wave 1 is
> only correct for some of the routes** — on the smaller-ring route it becomes the wrong part, and
> on the 2× route so does the Lightworks spider. Full analysis and the gearing menu:
> `T20_Build_Options.md` → *Shifter / Rear Derailleur* and *🔧 Gearing configurations*.

### Wave 4 — 🔮 after the bike is running
Child seats (Yepp Mini front, Yepp Nexxt 2 Maxi rear) · lights · mudguards · the Future Upgrade
Shortlist.
